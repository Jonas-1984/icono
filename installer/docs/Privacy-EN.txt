icono - Privacy notice
======================

In short: icono does not collect, send or share any data about you. The program does not connect to the internet on its own.

1. Controller
   Jonas Shojaei, www.bitbins.de, e-mail: info@bitbins.de

2. What icono does NOT do
   - no internet connection, no transfer to me or to third parties
   - no usage statistics, no telemetry, no trackers, no advertising
   - no user account, no cloud, no sign-in
   - no camera, microphone or location access

3. What is stored on your device (locally only)
   Folder: %LocalAppData%\Icono  (in Explorer: Windows key + R, then type %LocalAppData%\Icono)
   - theme.txt        your chosen colour theme (one word)
   - recent.txt       the list of recently opened files (file paths, so that "Open Recent" works)
   - Library\         your own icon library, if you put icons there
   - crash.log        an error log when the program reports an unexpected error (time, error message and technical call list;
                      the message can contain file names or paths)
   This data does not leave your device. I only receive it if you send me the error log voluntarily (for example by e-mail).
   icono saves your drawings only where you choose (File > Save / Export).

4. Deleting
   When you uninstall, the installer asks whether this local data should be deleted as well. You can delete the folder yourself at any time
   without damaging icono.

5. Links in the Help menu
   The entries "Documentation", "Check for Updates", "Website" and "Contact" open your browser or e-mail program when you click them. Only then
   a connection is made, by those programs. Their privacy terms and those of the visited site apply.

6. If you write to me
   If you write to me (info@bitbins.de) I process your e-mail address and your message to answer you and to fix the reported problem (Art. 6(1)(b)
   and (f) GDPR). I do not pass it on and delete it when the request is done and no legal retention duty exists. Please do not send drawings that
   contain personal data of third parties.

7. Your rights
   You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to lodge
   a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. Write to info@bitbins.de.

Version: October 2026. If the behaviour of the program changes (for example an update check), this notice will be adjusted beforehand.
