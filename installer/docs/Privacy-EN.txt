icono - Privacy notice
======================

In short: icono does not collect, send or share any data about you. The Windows program does not connect to the internet on its own. The Android
tablet app uses the internet in one case only: the first time you use "Convert to text" (handwriting to text) it loads the handwriting model of
your language once (about 20 MB, from Google, see 3). Your drawings and your handwriting are never sent. This notice covers the Windows program and
the Android tablet app.

1. Controller
   Jonas Shojaei, www.bitbins.de, e-mail: info@bitbins.de

2. What icono does NOT do
   - no transfer of your drawings or your handwriting to me or to third parties; no internet connection (Windows), and on the tablet only the
     one-time loading of the handwriting model
   - no usage statistics, no telemetry, no trackers, no advertising
   - no user account, no cloud, no sign-in
   - no camera, microphone or location access

3. What is stored on your device (locally only)
   Windows program - folder: %LocalAppData%\Icono  (in Explorer: Windows key + R, then type %LocalAppData%\Icono)
   - theme.txt        your chosen colour theme (one word)
   - recent.txt       the list of recently opened files (file paths, so that "Open Recent" works)
   - Library\         your own icon library, if you put icons there
   - crash.log        an error log when the program reports an unexpected error (time, error message and technical call list;
                      the message can contain file names or paths)
   This data does not leave your device. I only receive it if you send me the error log voluntarily (for example by e-mail).
   icono saves your drawings only where you choose (File > Save / Export).

   Android tablet app - in the private storage of the app, which other apps cannot read:
   - icono-folder.txt a reference to the folder you chose for your drawings (so that you need not choose it again)
   - exports\         a temporary copy of your last export (PDF or PNG), only so that it can be handed to the app you pick in "Share";
                      the next export replaces it
   The tablet app keeps no theme, no list of recent files, no own library and no error log. It keeps the language of your handwriting (icono-handwriting.txt)
   and, once you have used "Convert to text", the handwriting model of that language (loaded by Google ML Kit, kept by Android in the app's private storage).
   The permission it asks for is the internet, and only for loading that model: your tablet then contacts Google's servers (your IP address is visible
   to them, as with any download; Google's terms apply). The Google component that loads the model (ML Kit) adds two technical permissions that Android's
   download scheduling uses: network state and keeping the device awake while the model loads. icono uses them for nothing else. (Two more permissions
   of that component, starting after a restart and running as a foreground service, are taken out of the app.) After that "Convert to text" works without internet; the writing is recognized on the tablet.
   Android gives icono
   access only to the folder you chose and to files you pick yourself. The app is set so that Android does not include its data in the
   automatic device backup (cloud backup). Your drawings are saved only where you choose (Save as, Export > Save...).
   When you tap "Share...", Android opens its share sheet: the file goes only to the app you pick there, and that app's privacy terms apply.

4. Deleting
   Windows: when you uninstall, the installer asks whether this local data should be deleted as well. You can delete the folder yourself at
   any time without damaging icono.
   Android tablet: uninstalling the app (Settings > Apps > icono > Uninstall) removes its data, or use Settings > Apps > icono > Storage >
   Clear data / Clear cache. Your drawings in your own folder stay until you delete them.

5. Links in the Help menu
   The entries "Check for Updates", "Website" and "Contact" of the Windows program open your browser or e-mail program when you click them.
   Only then a connection is made, by those programs. Their privacy terms and those of the visited site apply.
   The user manual (Help > User Manual) and the notes in the "About icono" window open locally from the installation folder, without internet.
   The tablet app has two of them: "Website" and "Contact" in its About window (tap the icono logo) open your browser or e-mail app when you tap them -
   only then a connection is made, by those apps. The terms, this notice and the licences in that window are shown locally.

6. If you write to me
   If you write to me (info@bitbins.de) I process your e-mail address and your message to answer you and to fix the reported problem (Art. 6(1)(b)
   and (f) GDPR). I do not pass it on and delete it when the request is done and no legal retention duty exists. Please do not send drawings that
   contain personal data of third parties.

7. Your rights
   You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to lodge
   a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. Write to info@bitbins.de.

Version: 17 November 2026 (now also for the Android tablet app). If the behaviour of the program changes (for example an update check), this notice will be adjusted beforehand.
